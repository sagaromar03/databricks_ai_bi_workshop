Step 1 · Create a free Databricks workspace

Get a no-cost, free-forever Databricks Free Edition workspace so you can run the notebook and build the tables. No credit card, no cloud account.

Free foreverFree Edition replaced Community Edition (retired Jan 2026). No time limit, with daily usage limits.
No card, no cloudYou don't need a credit card or an AWS/Azure/GCP account to sign up.
Serverless & readyComes with serverless compute, Unity Catalog and default storage — start immediately.
Open the Free Edition sign-up page ↗

Create your workspace

  1. Go to the Free Edition page

    Visit databricks.com/learn/free-edition and click Sign up for Free Edition. (Direct sign-up: signup.databricks.com/?provider=DB_FREE_TIER.)

  2. Enter your details

    Provide name, email and organization/school. A personal email is fine — you don't need a business address.

  3. Choose a sign-up method

    Continue with Google/Microsoft or with email + password. Databricks then provisions a workspace for you automatically.

  4. Verify your email

    Open the verification email from Databricks and click the link (set a password if you chose the email method).

  5. Launch your workspace

    Log in — you land in the Data Intelligence Platform workspace. Serverless compute is already available, so there's no cluster to configure.

Good to know: Free Edition is serverless-only and quota-limited. Once your workspace is up, move on to loading the notebook and building the tables.
