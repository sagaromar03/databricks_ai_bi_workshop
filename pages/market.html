Whose data is this?

A quick gut-check before you dig in. fact_trade looks like a list of trades — but whose? Every row is one deal between two participants, so there is no single “us”. Pick a company below and watch where it turns up.

Trace a company across the tape:
#TraderCounterpartyCommodityPrice
Selected company as the trader Selected company as the counterparty
The tell: the company you picked is the trader on some rows and the counterparty on others. A single firm’s own ledger never does that — it is always one side of every deal. Seeing every name on both sides is the signature of a market-wide tape, the view an exchange or regulator has.
