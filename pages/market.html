Whose data is this?

A market-wide trade tape — not one company's books. Every row is one deal between two of the market's participants; there is no single "us". Hover a company to see its link to the market.

The tell: the same company can be the trader on one row and the counterparty on another — the classic sign of exchange / regulator data, not a single firm's ledger.
