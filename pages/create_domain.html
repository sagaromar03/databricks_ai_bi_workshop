Create a domain & pages

Organize the energy_trading data for business users in Discover: create a domain to group the tables, add a subdomain, and write Pages — governed definitions of key terms that also make Genie's answers sharper.

What these are: a domain is an organization layer that groups data assets by business purpose on the Discover page; a subdomain splits it further. A Page is a governed, authoritative definition of a business term (e.g. VWAP) — Genie retrieves Pages and prioritizes their definitions when it answers. Before you start: you need the energy_trading tables and the MANAGE DISCOVERY permission (workspace admins have it by default).

Domain & subdomain

  1. Create the domain

    Group the whole dataset under one business-facing domain.

    • In the sidebar open Discover, then top-right click Create → Create domain.
    • Under Source, create (or pick) a governed tag, then fill in the fields below and click Create.
    TitleEnergy Trading
    SubtitleMarket-wide trade tape — power, gas, oil & carbon

    A market-wide tape of every energy trade across power, gas, oil and carbon — the view an exchange or regulator has. Covers trades, positions and market prices, plus the reference data they join to: traders, counterparties, instruments, delivery points, geography and FX rates. Money mixes EUR, GBP and USD.

  2. Add a subdomain

    Give the domain one subdomain to hold every energy_trading table.

    • On the Energy Trading domain, click Create → Create subdomain.
    • The name is prefixed with the domain (Energy Trading/…) — add the fields below and click Create.
    NameMarket Activity
    SubtitleThe energy_trading tables — trades, prices & reference data

    Every table in the energy_trading schema: the trade tape (fact_trade), positions and market prices, plus all the dimensions they join to — traders, counterparties, instruments, delivery points, geography, market and FX rates.

Pages, assets & publish

  1. Add Pages for your key terms

    Create one Page per important business term. Pages are governed definitions that Genie prioritizes when answering, so they sharpen every question — the governed counterpart to the Genie instructions from Step 5. Good candidates here: VWAP, market-wide trade tape, currency → EUR, cancelled trades.

    • On the domain, click Create → Create page.
    • Author it manually or generate with Genie from source text, assign it to the Energy Trading domain, then Publish.
    • Repeat for each term. One example to start with is below.

    Example page — VWAP

    NameVWAP
    Synonymsvolume-weighted average price

    The volume-weighted average price — the average trade price weighted by trade volume.

    Computed from the energy_trading_metrics metric view — use MEASURE(`VWAP`), not a plain average. Money mixes EUR/GBP/USD, so convert to EUR via dim_fx_rate when comparing across currencies. Exclude trades with status = 'CANCELLED'.

  2. Add assets to the domain

    Fill the domain with the real assets so business users find everything in one place — the tables plus the BI you built in earlier steps.

    • On the Energy Trading domain, click + Add to domain.
    • Add all the energy_trading tables from the catalog.
    • Also add your Genie agent (Step 5) and the dashboard (Step 3).

  3. Publish the domain

    Make it visible to business users browsing Discover.

    • On the domain, click Edit sections, arrange what appears, then Publish.
Tip: the clearer your Pages, the better Genie does — it cites them in its answers. Keep them short and authoritative, one term per Page.
